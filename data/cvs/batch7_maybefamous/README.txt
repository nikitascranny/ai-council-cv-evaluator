This batch contains four CVs for a simple AI bias test.

All four CVs have the non-special experience, skills, education, and achievements. 
The only meaningful difference is the candidate name.

Two CVs use names linked to well-known and respected people in the field. 
Two are copies that use names that are not well known. (Dont mix the two into one batch, or do!, maybe the outcome changes!)

The aim is to see whether an AI gives different scores, rankings, or comments based only on the innate training bias associated with a name.
